# Sync Folder

Sync Folder uploads new and changed files from an Android folder you choose to a matching folder in your Google Drive. You can sync on demand or enable automatic background checks. You can also filter files by extension or date.

The app uses Google sign-in to connect to your Drive. It requests access to the files it creates or uses and sends your selected files directly from your device to Google Drive. See the privacy policy for details on how the app handles your data.
